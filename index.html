Frequently asked questions

Common Questions About Our Services

Here are the answers to the questions we hear most often from construction, energy, and utility teams.

Do you understand construction?

Yes. For 10+ years I was responsible for the communications for multi-million-dollar capital improvement programs covering residential and commercial construction, working with owners, contractors and the public. I’ve also built brands and websites for construction and energy companies including Precision Wire Electric, Indaspec, and more.

Will this bring in more of the right projects?

That’s the goal, and we’ll help you measure it. I start by defining your ideal projects and clients, then build your website, online presence and content to reach your target buyers. We set up tracking from day one so you can see inquiry sources and which ones lead to business.

Will the right people find and trust us?

Yes, if they can find your proof. Owners, developers, GCs and facility managers check your website, Google profile and LinkedIn before they call, and more of them now use AI tools to research. I make sure your projects, capabilities, safety record and reviews show up clearly in all of those places, including AI search results.

Is the investment clear and worth it?

Yes. Every engagement has a fixed scope, timeline and price agreed before we start, with no surprise fees. Packages start at $2,500 for a website, and support starts at $150 a month. Because a single construction project can be worth more than a year of marketing, we track results against your average project value so you can see the return.

Can you run this without overloading our team?

Yes. Up front, I collaborate with your team to agree on owner responsibilities, deadlines and approvals, so nothing stalls and nobody gets surprised by extra work. We hold a kick-off meeting to decide on topics such as strategy, writing, design and setup and implementation of your digital marketing assets.
