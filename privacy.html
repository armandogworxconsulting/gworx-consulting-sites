Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to personal information when you visit gworxconsulting.com (the “site”), operated by G-WORX Consulting (“G-WORX”, “we”, “I”). It covers the public website only. Work carried out under a signed engagement is governed by that engagement’s own agreement.

The short version

The site is a single static page. It has no contact form, no account system, no advertising and no advertising, and we do not sell or share personal information. It does use Google Tag Manager to measure how the site is used, which sets cookies in your browser. Two other things involve Google as well: the home page embeds a YouTube video, and booking a consultation happens on Google’s scheduling service rather than on this site. All of this is described below.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you select Free Consultation, you are taken to a Google Calendar appointment page. Anything you enter there — typically your name, email address and any notes about your project — is submitted to Google and forwarded to us so we can hold the meeting. We use it to schedule and prepare for the consultation, and to follow up about the work you asked about.

If you email us directly, we receive whatever you choose to put in that message.

Information collected automatically

This site loads Google Tag Manager (container GTM-PFH7GZPV), which measures how visitors find and use the site — pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, and which links were followed. To do that it sets cookies in your browser, and the data is processed by Google. We use it to understand which pages and channels bring in enquiries, not to identify you personally.

You can opt out by blocking cookies for this site in your browser, by using a tracking-blocker extension, or with Google’s own opt-out add-on (opens in a new tab). The site works normally either way.

Our hosting provider also records standard server logs — IP address, time of request, requested file, browser user agent — which exist for security and troubleshooting and are not used to build a profile of you.

The embedded YouTube video is a separate matter. It is served in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which means YouTube does not store cookies for ad personalisation when the video merely loads. It does still receive your IP address and browser details, and once you press play it may store data in your browser to support playback. We receive none of that and cannot see who watched.

Third-party services

Four third-party services are involved in serving or measuring this site, all of them Google:

  • Google — the consultation booking page is hosted by Google. Your use of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).
  • Google Tag Manager — loads the measurement tag described above and sets cookies in your browser. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).
  • YouTube — the overview video on the home page is hosted by YouTube and loaded from youtube-nocookie.com. Playback is governed by the same Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).
  • Google Fonts — the Inter typeface is loaded from Google’s font servers, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google receives your IP address and user agent as part of that request.

We do not control what these providers do with the information they receive. If this changes — for example if analytics or a contact form is added — this policy will be updated before the change goes live.

How long we keep information

Booking and email correspondence is kept for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and to meet our tax and record-keeping obligations, then deleted. Server logs are retained on our host’s standard schedule.

How information is protected

The site is served over HTTPS. Correspondence and booking records are held in access-controlled accounts. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to certain uses of it. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have no financial incentive programmes. To make a request, email us using the address below; we may need to verify your identity before we act.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your information, can go to armando@gworxconsulting.com.
